## Study details

**Principal Investigators:** Dr. Michael Liut, Assistant Professor at the University of Toronto

**Study Title:** Perceptions of AI Contribution and Attribution in Higher Education in Computing Education

**Supported By:** University of Toronto

## What is this form?

This form is called a Consent Form. It gives you information about the study so you can make an informed decision about participating in this research. We encourage you to take time to think it over and ask questions now or at any other time. If you decide to participate, you will be asked to sign this form and you will be given a copy for your records.

## Important things to know

- You can choose not to take part.
- You can agree to take part and later change your mind.
- Your decision will not be held against you.

## Why are we doing this research study?

We aim to understand how students and instructional staff perceive the use of AI in a range of academic scenarios and deliverables. The findings will help inform AI attribution norms in education and support the design of better human-computer interaction tools and practices.

## Who can participate?

Students or instructional staff who are at least 18 years old and have experience using LLMs may participate in this research study. We anticipate that 200 to 300 people will participate.

## What will I be asked to do?

The survey should take about **30 minutes** to complete. The **first 150 qualified student participants** who complete the survey and **pass all attention checks** will receive a **$5 gift card**. In addition, qualified student completers will be entered into a raffle for **5 gift cards worth $30 each**. If you receive compensation, you will be contacted separately using the email address you provide.

Compensation will be provided only after successful completion of the survey, passing all attention checks, and confirmation that appropriate effort has been demonstrated. Please read each question carefully and answer to the best of your ability. Attention checks are included throughout the survey, and failure to complete them correctly will result in forfeiture of compensation.

## Benefits and risks

You are unlikely to receive any direct benefit from participating other than receiving compensation if you qualify.

This study does not involve risks beyond those encountered in daily life. The experience should be comparable to viewing a computer monitor and using a keyboard. You may withdraw at any time.

## Privacy and data protection

We will make efforts to protect the privacy and security of your personal information throughout the study. The survey is administered online through reVisit. De-identified data from this study may be shared among research teams at the University of Toronto. Only individuals who require access for research purposes, such as members of the research team, will review your study-related records.

## Questions?

If you have questions about this project or experience a research-related problem, you may contact the Principal Investigator, Dr. Michael Liut, at michael.liut@utoronto.ca (Department of Mathematical and Computational Sciences, University of Toronto Mississauga), or lead PhD student Harry Ye at harryye@cs.toronto.edu.

If you have questions about your rights as a research participant, you may contact the Human Research Ethics Unit at ethics.review@utoronto.ca or 416-946-3273.

## What if I change my mind?

You can decide not to participate, or you can start and later decide to leave the research at any time, and it will not be held against you. To do so, simply exit the experiment. Any data collected before submission will not be saved.
