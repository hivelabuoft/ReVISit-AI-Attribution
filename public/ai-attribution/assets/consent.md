<h2 style="margin-top: 0; font-size: 1.1rem; color: #2c3e50;">Why are we doing this research study?</h2>
<p style="margin-bottom: 0.5em;">We aim to understand how students engage with large language-model (LLM) coding tools and to assess the tools' effectiveness in supporting the learning process. The findings will inform the design of future tools that more effectively support students' coding practices.</p>
<p style="margin-bottom: 0;">We aim to understand how students and instructional staff perceive the use of AI in various scenarios as part of a range of different deliverables. The finding will inform the broader educational practitioner in setting norms of AI attribution, and human-computer interaction researchers in develop better interactions.</p>
</div>

<div style="background: #f5f7fa; border-left: 4px solid #4a90d9; border-radius: 6px; padding: 1em 1.4em; margin: 1.5em 0;">
<h2 style="margin-top: 0; font-size: 1.1rem; color: #2c3e50;">Who can participate?</h2>
<p style="margin-bottom: 0;">Any students or instructional staff who are at least 18 years of age and have experience using LLM may participate in this research study. We anticipate that 200-300 people will participate.</p>
</div>
