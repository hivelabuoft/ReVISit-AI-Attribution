📖 Original Vignette Text

In a third-year mobile development course, students learn about platform-specific design guidelines and user experience best practices for iOS and Android. The instructor assigns a planning task to generate a complete set of wireframe descriptions for a fitness tracking app. The student knows what features are needed but uses an AI tool to generate the screen-by-screen layout descriptions. The student rejects the AI's initial generic dashboard, arguing it feels too corporate. They then directed the AI to completely shift the layout concept from a standard list to a gamified 'adventure map' style. The AI regenerates the complete design document based on these specific constraints. The student reviews the revised plan, finds it satisfactory, and submits the text-based wireframe descriptions as the final design artifact. The work is evaluated on the logical flow and completeness of the user interface plan.
