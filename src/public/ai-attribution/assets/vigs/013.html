📖 Original Vignette Text

In a final-year capstone course, students work in teams to propose, design, and build a significant software system over a semester. The first milestone is a project proposal document that outlines the problem statement, proposed solution, and high-level architecture. The student is not sure how to proceed, and asks an AI agent to generate a full proposal for a 'Smart Campus Navigation App'. The AI produces a comprehensive three-page document including a feature list, a technology stack, and a Gantt chart. The student reads through the generated proposal, and submits it exactly as generated without editing the timeline or the feature set. The submission will be reviewed by the professor to determine if the project is viable for the term.
