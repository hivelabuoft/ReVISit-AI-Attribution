📖 Original Vignette Text

In a third-year systems course, students examine the trade-offs between various CPU scheduling algorithms like Round Robin and Shortest Job First. The instructor assigns a theoretical question requiring students to design a scheduling policy that minimizes response time for interactive tasks. The student understands the material well but wants to verify the specific theoretical advantages of the new proposed algorithm, called "Multilevel Feedback Queues", before writing their answer. They ask the AI to explain the theoretical justification for using feedback queues in this context. The AI provides a detailed explanation highlighting the prioritization of short bursts and the aging mechanism. The student writes an answer that follows the AI's reasoning and adds no additional details. The completed response is submitted as part of a written homework assignment.
