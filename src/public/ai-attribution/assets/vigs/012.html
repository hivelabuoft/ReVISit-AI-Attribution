📖 Original Vignette Text

In a fourth-year parallel systems course, students focus on concurrency control, mutual exclusion, and avoiding race conditions in multi-threaded applications. The instructor requires a design snippet showing how to use a condition variable to manage a producer-consumer buffer. The student understands the pattern well but asks the AI to generate a standard boilerplate for the lock acquisition sequence. The AI produces a correct standard block of C-like pseudocode. The student takes this block and significantly rewrites it to add extra guard checks required by the assignment's constraints. They also manually insert detailed comments explaining the invariants at each step, which were not present in the AI output. The final design artifact is a highly customized logic block that integrates the AI's syntax with the student's complex logic.
