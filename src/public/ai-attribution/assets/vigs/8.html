📝 The Assignment

  • Third-year introductory database course
  • Task: submit a design plan for a customer order database
  • Goal: structure tables to achieve 3NF (splitting tables to reduce redundancy)

😕 The Starting Point

  • Student does not understand the difference between normalization forms
  • Unsure how to separate the address data

🤖 First AI Interaction

  • AI suggests separating street, city, and zip code into many separate tables
  • Student rejects this solution — finds it too complex to understand

🔄 Iterating with the AI

  • Student prompts the AI for simpler alternatives
  • Multiple rounds of iteration occur
  • Process continues until reaching a structure that feels manageable

🚀 Writing & Submission

  • Student writes their schema design plan
  • Text is based on the final explanation provided by the AI
  • Design document is submitted as a PDF
  • Teaching assistant will review the submission

📖 Original Vignette Text

In a third-year introductory database course, the instructor requires students to submit a design plan for a customer order database, detailing how tables should be structured to achieve 3NF (a rule for splitting tables to reduce redundancy). The student does not understand the difference and is unsure how to separate the address data. The student initially rejects an AI solution that separates the street, city, and zip code into too many separate tables, finding it too complex to understand. The student prompts the AI for simpler alternatives, iterating through multiple rounds until reaching a structure that feels manageable. The student then writes their schema design plan, basing their text on the final explanation provided by the AI. The resulting design document is submitted as a PDF for the teaching assistant to review.
