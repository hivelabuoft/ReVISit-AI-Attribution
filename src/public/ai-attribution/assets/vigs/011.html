📖 Original Vignette Text

In a second-year data science course, students learn to design reproducible pipelines for cleaning and preprocessing raw datasets (ETL). The assignment asks for a pseudocode description of a function to handle missing values in a pandas DataFrame. The student knows the logic of mean imputation but wants a clean, Pythonic pseudocode representation. The student seeks help from AI but rejects its initial approach, knowing it introduces bias, and prompts for a 'flagging' strategy instead. When the AI offers a complex statistical fix, the student restricts the tool again, specifying a simple 'if-then' logic, suitable for a flowchart. After three rounds of tightening constraints, the AI generates a concise logic block that matches the student's vision. The student adopts this specific version without further edits and pastes it into their pipeline design document. The plan is submitted for approval before the implementation phase.
