📖 Original Vignette Text

In a third-year software design course, the syllabus covers high-level architectural patterns such as Model-View-Controller (MVC) and Microservices. The assignment asks students to select and justify an architectural pattern for a hypothetical large-scale e-commerce platform. The student is knowledgeable about patterns but wants to explore the specific trade-offs of using an event-driven architecture versus a monolithic one. They enter a discussion with the AI, initially rejecting its generic comparison for missing key risks on latency. The student iterates through multiple prompts, instructing the AI to reconstruct its argument to prioritize performance over maintenance costs. They write their justification document, adopting the AI's final reasoning exactly without adding further critique. The design justification is uploaded to the course management system for grading.
