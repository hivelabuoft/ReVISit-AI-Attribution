📖 Original Vignette Text

In a second year data structures course, the instructor requires a text-based proposal selecting the best sorting strategy for a large dataset. Unsure which method is most efficient, the student asks the AI to recommend a specific algorithm. The AI suggests a divide-and-conquer approach and outlines its unique characteristics. Using the AI's response as the primary source, the student writes their proposal by integrating the AI's recommendation and rationale without modification, adopting the suggested strategy in their plan directly.
