📖 Original Vignette Text

In a database management course, students practice writing commands to extract specific data patterns from large datasets. The assignment requires writing a query to identify customers who have purchased at least one item from every available product category. The student is comfortable with basic database concepts and operations, but wants to verify the logic for this specific 'all-inclusive' check. They ask an AI chatbot to explain the conceptual steps for comparing a customer's purchase history against the full product list. The AI provides a clear text-based explanation suggesting a method. The student follows this logic precisely, writing the code step-by-step as described by the AI as is. The completed query is saved and run against the course database for grading.
